# Armageddon Templar classes and reusable magic items

This package adds the Jihaen, Lirathan, and Southern Templar roles through an additive role manifest and an independently owned `Role Skill Progression` merit. The frozen mundane guild and subguild TSVs remain unchanged. The installer never enrols existing characters.

## Eligibility and activation

Each role availability prog combines a separate zero-argument `*TemplarEnabled()` prog with its class rule. The enablement progs are seeded `false`, so builder activation is explicit. Jihaen defaults to male chargen characters and Lirathan to female characters; builders can edit the role eligibility prog while retaining the separate enablement gate. Southern eligibility is unrestricted by gender. Activating a role does not grant city-clan membership, authority to control gems, military protection, a location, or any runtime power by itself.

## Northern skill trees

The class manifest transcribes the supplied Jihaen and Lirathan MHT archives. Published opening, cap, and go-on values are retained. Parent links follow the indentation in the archived skill lists. Jihaen Pressure Points (20 opening, 90 cap) is an inferred trait required by the native poke implementation. Cathexis, Silent Psi, Tatlum, and Magick Sense are additive skill identities because they are absent from the existing canonical Armageddon skill inventory; their published/proposed class openings and caps remain explicit in the manifest.

The native cap formulas combine the installed mundane and Templar source role rules. Both seeder passes retain Templar caps on rerun and preserve builder-edited formulas. Spoken-language skills retain the canonical 200-point scale.

Templar primary roles also compose with mundane subclasses through the shared progression service, retaining primary caps and prerequisites while applying the subclass opening bonus once.

Analyze follows the existing mundane-class adaptation: its source values remain in the manifest, while the native Analyze eligibility check grants the capability to the three Templar roles. It does not add a parallel Analyze skill. The owning check keeps builder overrides on rerun.

`poke target location` uses the registered auxiliary action `Jihaen Pressure Point`. The eight supported locations are neck (agility impairment), spine (paralysis), throat (speech blocking), stomach (severe nausea), and left/right arms and legs (temporary loss of function). Only the location/effect intent for the neck is explicit in the article. Durations, penalties, additional locations, resistance, and cost are native implementation defaults and should remain builder-configurable.

### Additional pressure points

Use `poke <target> stomach`, `poke <target> left arm`, `poke <target> right arm`, `poke <target> left leg`, or `poke <target> right leg`. Compact forms such as `leftarm` and quoted forms such as `"left arm"` also work. These five additions are authored speculation.

A stomach strike adds a persistent physical nausea cause for 45 seconds at normalized intensity 4. At stock settings this gives approximately a -6.67 check penalty. It feeds the ordinary nausea system, combines with existing causes and respects nausea resistance/neutralisation. Native vomiting checks every 35 seconds depend on hunger/thirst; vomiting can spend 50 stamina and reduce water and satiation. Applying or expiring the cause refreshes nausea immediately without an extra metabolism or drug-consequence tick. Existing nausea from other causes remains after the poke expires.

Each limb strike disables exactly the selected limb for five seconds. Its parts cannot perform their ordinary attacks, holding/wielding or movement functions during that interval. Held or wielded items using that limb are dropped once, including a shared two-handed grip. Leg strikes invoke the existing posture rules; those rules decide whether the character falls or can continue with other limbs/aids. Recovery restores function but does not collect items or stand the character automatically. Distinct limbs can be affected together, while repeating the same limb or stomach poke neither stacks nor refreshes its duration.

Anatomy aliases resolve a unique current limb with the required type and side; missing or ambiguous anatomy is refused. The queued action captures body/limb identity and revalidates it at impact. Stomach strikes require abdominal anatomy and a stomach organ. The original three locations retain their existing rules and defaults. Every location uses the same opposed attack, free functioning hand, 5 stamina cost, 3-second delay, and shared 15-second cooldown.

The `pressurepoint` auxiliary effect builder accepts `stomach <seconds>`, `leftarm <seconds>`, `rightarm <seconds>`, `leftleg <seconds>`, `rightleg <seconds>`, and `nauseaintensity <value>`, plus `anatomy <location> <aliases...>`. XML stores the corresponding `*seconds` and `nauseaintensity` attributes; missing fields in older definitions use the stock defaults. Zero durations disable the configured effect. Reseeding preserves builder-edited XML. The reusable `LimbNumbness` and `MundaneNausea` timed effects save their limb identity or normalized intensity and remaining duration using ordinary effect persistence, with no schema migration.

## Southern class

Southern Templar progression copies the existing Warrior role rather than changing the frozen Warrior TSV. Its separate casting capability grants Sense Enchantment and Pierce Concealment permanently, plus three replaceable selections from the Detection, Combat, Movement, and Alteration pools. The pools use the canonical stock identities Read Attunement, Sense Toxin, See the Unbodied; Ember Lance, Shroud Eyes, Heavy Slumber; Waystep, Draw Traveller, Join Traveller; Mend Flesh, Stone Skin, Seal Voice. Grade 7, shouted invocation, zero-cost casting and reserve refill inside configured territory are setting policy choices; these spell identifiers and their arrangement are inferred rather than claims about the archive.

## Reusable shrines, binding gems, and policies

A shrine is an item with the generic `SpellSelectionShrine` component and a serialized `ConditionalMagicItemPolicy` of kind `SpellSelectionShrine`. Its policy names the capability, selection limit, choices/categories, eligibility, and replacement behavior. The Armageddon shrine configures three choices for a Southern Templar capability; other games may configure unrelated traditions and spell lists with the same component. Class activation, military authority, and Allanak territory all begin as false editable progs. Southern Templar activation also requires the character's Southern Templar casting capability.

A binding gem uses generic `ConditionalBinding` content. It applies to the eight configured elementalist capabilities; Defiler is an incompatible wearer and destroys the gem on wear. Southern Templars must have the enabled class prog, the Southern casting capability, configured military authority, and the regalia item worn to exercise control. A control pulse checks the gem every 40/12 seconds with 5/12 seconds of jitter and a 50% activation chance. Outside the configured Allanak territory, an unescorted gem wearer takes 10 health, 25 stun, and 25 stamina loss, with a 10-health floor and collapse; an authorised escort suppresses that pulse. Control deals 20 health, 40 stun, and 40 stamina damage with a 10-health floor and collapse. Removal deals 15 health, 20 stun, and 30 stamina damage, with a 10-health floor and collapse. The gem intercepts spells directed at configured military members using the manifest's explicit 27-spell harm/control list. This is authored setting policy: there is no universal hostility flag on spell effects. Builders can change the spell IDs or configure mood matching for their own content. These are Armageddon policy values, not defaults embedded in the component implementation. Defiler wear applies one maximum health pool of damage, clamped to the remaining health, destroys the gem, and uses native incapacitation. The item is recognised by its typed component identity, never by descriptive text.

Resource restoration and item issuance likewise use the generic `ResourceRestorer` and `ItemIssuer` component kinds. The Templar reserve has a cap of 100, starts at zero, and has no passive generator; a medallion holds six uses and refills only inside the configured territory. A robe carrying the configured regalia item-group identity issues binding gems while worn. Southern military authority and Allanak territory remain explicit false-by-default builder bindings; leaving them unset grants no control or recharge. Adding a class alone grants no authority.

## Source and adaptation ledger

- Jihaen and Lirathan tree values and parent ordering: supplied Fandom MHT archives. Lirathan page states it is current as of 2003. [Southern source](https://armageddonmud.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Templar) supplies shrine/automatic shouting/reserve behavior; the supplied C amulet handler specifies six charges, which takes precedence over the article's four.
- Southern Templar class policy: source description plus the approved warrior-with-spells interpretation; spell pool and shrine choices are designed for this native setting.
- Jihaen poke: article documents `poke target location`, agility reduction at the neck, paralysis, and muting. It does not identify the latter two body locations or any durations/balancing values.
- C source follow-up: no additional Jihaen pole/poke effects were identified. The stomach and four limb effects are explicitly speculative additions approved for this adaptation, not historical source claims.
- Gem source behaviour was mapped to reusable generic policies. Health/stun/stamina values are native-unit adaptations, not claims that the source used the same unit system. Do not hard-code Allanak room IDs, clan IDs, factions, or location names.

## Installation and reruns

The Templar manifest is a separate module and embeds no changes to existing elemental or Sorcerer rosters. Seeded role/prog/item configuration ownership must be reconciled by stable keys and preserve builder edits and stable identities on rerun. Fresh installations and reruns keep all three classes and Southern casting disabled until a builder edits their activation bindings. Existing characters are not enrolled.

The module requires the canonical Armageddon skill package. Older magic-only installations retain their content and report this prerequisite instead of installing incomplete class trees. Relational installation commits the Templar graph and its ownership together. Custom incompatible prog signatures are retained and reported for explicit builder resolution.

## Player commands and builder configuration

Use `itempower <shrine> list` and `itempower <shrine> select <spell IDs or quoted names>` to replace the complete current selection, up to the shrine allowance. Permanent spells do not consume selections. The shrine must be accessible in its configured territory. Selections belong to the canonical character and the chosen capability, survive restart, and never promote an independently acquired spell route. Patron routes reject practice, overreach and charged-device production. Grade seven is expressed by `mon`; normal casting automatically emits the required shouted formula using the spell's authored language and categories.

The Southern capability supplies fourteen route-local incantations in Sirihish, labelled FutureMUD aliases. These fill absent spell incantations without changing shared canonical spells or granting vocabulary to other casting routes. A spell's own incantation takes precedence. Builders edit a fallback with `casting patron incantation <spell> <language> <reach> <element> <sphere> <mood> [energy] [difficulty]`, or remove it with `<spell> none`. Named casts and complete spoken formulas share the same language, target, route and payment validation.

Use `itempower <medallion> recharge` in the configured territory, then `itempower <medallion> use` outside it to restore the reserve completely. New medallions start uncharged. A full reserve spends no charge. The charge bank and resource amounts use the engine's ordinary dirty-save batch. Splitting stacks or merging items cannot duplicate the charge bank.

Reducing a restorer's capacity through a component revision clamps existing excess charges, including during cold loading of a pending revision transition. Increasing capacity grants no charges.

Wear the configured regalia to use `itempower <regalia> issue <gem prototype ID or quoted name>` and `itempower <regalia> shock <online character>`. The target must actually wear an eligible binding of the configured group. Only controller authority, the required worn regalia and the configured range grant control; descriptions and item names confer none. Gem removal is prohibited for ordinary users and intended removal attempts apply the configured consequence once. Administrator removal remains available.

All four component types can be created and revised through the normal item component builder. Their shared fields include `eligibility`, `controller`, `territory`, `escort`, `protected`, `incompatible`, `regalia`, `group`, `range`, `wearlocations`, `outside`, `removallock`, `hostiles`, `interceptspells`, `interceptmoods`, `interval`, `jitter`, `chance`, `delay`, `worn`, `capability`, `limit`, `replacement`, `choice`, `unchoice`, `resource`, `capacity`, and `issue`. Progs take one character and return boolean. Consequences have independent health, stun and stamina amounts, a health floor, collapse/destruction flags and a message. `activationfractions`, `removalfractions`, `periodicfractions` and `incompatiblewearfractions` configure additional fractions of maximum health/stun/stamina. These policies can describe unrelated shrines, oath bindings or reserve items in another setting without engine changes.

Builders must separately enable the Southern casting capability and configure the `SouthernTemplarMilitaryAuthority`, `SouthernTemplarAllanakTerritory`, and class enablement progs. Configure `LirathanTemplarInTuluk` for the upkeep exemption. Their initial false results deliberately make an unconfigured installation unavailable. Ordinary role availability retains builder edits on rerun, as do edited component policies and deliberately removed role-merit/component links.

## Lirathan abilities and native adaptations

The Lirathan capability binds twenty independently unlocked powers to their source skills. `LPSI HEAR` receives linked mental traffic; `LPSI THOUGHTSENSE` and `LPSI EMPATHY` receive local thoughts and feelings respectively. Each has a distinct `end...` command. `LPSI SILENTPSI` conceals mental identity across schools and suppresses passive activity announcements; it makes retained traces harder to identify rather than deleting history. `LPSI LOCATE` renders a remote room using the observer's perception. Masquerade sends a private, sanitised sensory vision; Illusion delivers room narration without creating physical objects. Control uses the existing restricted forced-command policy and respects ignore-force.

Cathexis establishes a chosen clan channel; `LPSI COMMUNE <message>` reaches eligible online members on the same plane. It ends on death, loss of usable consciousness, clan membership or power access. Dome blocks crossing mental traffic, ends existing cross-boundary mind contacts, suppresses passive sensing in the ward, and blocks/collapses walking shadows. The deterministic dome mapping replaces source probability rolls.

Mindblast damages one quarter of maximum health and stun. Mindwipe drains one quarter of maximum stun and stamina, blocks spellcasting and learning, and penalises skill-based checks (including derived/theoretical skills) by 25. Attribute-only physiological checks retain their values. Its duration is `clamp(floor(skill/10)-1, 1, 8) * 600` seconds. Consequences can overwhelm remaining stun and incapacitate the recipient; voluntary bodily-cost admission retains its safe limits. Unsupported health strategies refuse rather than inventing a damage conversion.

Shadowwalk uses an independently owned, same-school backing spell and the generic Walking Shadow lifecycle. `LPSI SHADOWWALK SELF` invokes its configured grade-seven copy, preserves primary identity and the anchored body, uses the authored Shadow plane and bounded travel policy, and collapses on logout/reboot. It does not grant an elemental spell. The generic spell-backed power builder accepts `grade <1-7|none>`; `none` retains its existing legacy power argument behavior.

Psionic activation uses the source integer formula based on learned skill and Willpower, plus a random 0-3 stun cost. `bodilycost` configures this on any power. `stunupkeep` configures the skill/Wisdom rolls and optional exemption prog. Maintenance aggregates sixty source second-ticks into the engine's minute heartbeat rather than adding a new per-character scheduler. Safe admission precedes invocation; actual activation cost follows the attempted effect through the existing power-payment lifecycle. This timing, projection boundaries, combat resistance, native health units and the conservative forced-command restrictions are explicit FutureMUD adaptations.
