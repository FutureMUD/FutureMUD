# Builder guide coverage review

18 November 2026. This review assesses the launch collection and the builder workflows in the thematic documentation indexes at `545479402`. It measures what a builder can learn from the documents. Command and gameplay examples retain the verification status recorded in their sources.

## Published guide coverage

Each guide should explain the objects and their relationships, document the commands and their effects, and walk through at least one complete setup with expected results. Describe dependencies and integration with other systems. Cover permissions, revisions or activation where relevant, plus observable failures and how to recover. Use the same standard for people and agents working in game.
