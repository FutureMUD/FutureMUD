Purpose
=======

Validate the replacement proximity-event system after the historical item-proximity workaround was removed. The test uses a real item anchor, an ordinary cell (where the correct co-located proximity band is Distant), and a non-administrator NPC that crosses into the anchor's cell.

Server-restart/hydration check
==============================

An initial restart exposed a real defect: loading a persisted position called SetTarget and therefore published a live proximity event before the target character's body and traits were fully reconstructed. The trap's spot check then threw a NullReferenceException during startup.

After adding load-hydration suppression, the local LabMUD process reached its ready line, the existing administrator connected to character 1, and `look` completed successfully in Cell #2669. No trap fired and no startup exception was logged.
