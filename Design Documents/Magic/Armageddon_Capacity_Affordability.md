# Armageddon capacity and affordability

Stage1C adds explicit native attribute capacity to the configurable engine. The stock
installer must still select a compatible mental/spiritual attribute and an authored
energy scale. Nothing in this checkpoint approves a world attribute mapping or fixed
balance coefficients. `ARM02 Agility` is the disposable harness attribute, not a stock
recommendation.

The optional resource configuration binds a native body attribute to `variable` in a
named trait expression, using raw or effective value explicitly. The historical-shaped
engineering scenario is:

```text
Min(12*variable,Max(2*variable,100+3*(variable-13)))
```

This does not import historical race/spice constants or a separate random capacity roll.
Its selected attribute, expression and basis persist in resource XML. A configured
canonical reserve uses its owner's current body; focus selection does not substitute
a second body's attributes. Maximum, available balance, passive entitlement and gathering
remain separate. Initial balances are zero. Capacity rises, repeated enrolment and route
reattachment grant no energy. Valid decreases clamp excess; invalid configurations retain
recoverable balances while casting, credits and debits refuse them.

Standard invalidation covers native attribute writes/addition/removal; actor/body trait
merits; trait bonus and psychic-suppression effects; inventory/implant cache changes;
implant power, damage/healing and component revisions; labour assignment/hours; race
attribute bonus edits; and mapped expression edits. Character reconstruction, nested
effect restoration and body transitions defer clamping until successful completion.
An incomplete reconstruction cannot credit or spend through the new attribute-cap path.
Custom modifiers or arbitrary cap Progs can depend on external state without discoverable
events; their mutation paths must notify capacity changes. Quotes evaluate without writes
and live accounting validates the current cap and actual funds.

Complete spell application batches target and caster modifiers per canonical owner.
Parent removal/expiration and aggregate removals use the same nested reconciliation scope.
With raw attribute 10, capacity `variable*10`, balance 90 and opposing +5/-5 children,
the completed operation preserves balance 90/capacity 100 in either child order. A genuine
final decrease clamps against the completed maximum; increases never refill. Live mutation
batching keeps ordinary resource effects usable while incomplete reconstruction still
refuses accounting. Configured-reserve credits and debits use actual funds and defer
upper-cap clamping until the live operation completes, after validating finite amounts
and the current capacity. An intervening +7 credit produces balance 97/capacity 100 in
either opposing-modifier order; final capacity 70 clamps to 70, and final capacity 150
retains a legitimate +50 credit at balance 140. Invalid capacities still refuse credits
and debits. Other resources retain immediate clamping.
Exceptions release the scope and parent removal guard; the remaining
actual effects determine capacity, without rollback or refunds.

## Provisional energy scenarios

[Scenario input](Armageddon_Capacity_Affordability_Scenarios.json) records all assumptions.
The chosen raw attributes 4/11.5/19 produce maxima 48/95.5/118. These values are engineering
probes, not approved stock ranges. The exact source roster supplies printed minima for
82 spells; other traditions' 72 additional candidates still require explicit cost authorship.

The generated CSV enumerates 82 spells × 28 normal grade pairs and six next-grade
Overreach pairs × three capacity scenarios × two profiles = **16,728 rows**. It uses the
production source curve and capacities evaluated on a real native body. Normal grades
are requested 1 through controlled 1–7; Overreach is only the exact next grade through 7,
with its additional raw-proficiency/difficulty gates. Secondary costs in these envelope
profiles are explicitly zero; real authored costs must still aggregate normally.

| Source reference | 1 | 1 | 75 | 1,129 | 0 / 0 |
| Provisional native fixture | 0.5 | 1.5 | 56.25 | 492 | 0 / 0 |

Lower pools cannot fund every otherwise legal cast. Both profiles provide an attainable
ordinary/advanced energy configuration for every covered spell/grade pair. The native
worst-minimum fixture quotes all 34 pairs without payment, then pays 25 for normal grade7
and 56.25 for grade7 Overreach through the non-admin command adapter. Another regression
explicitly demonstrates 112.5 source plus 10 secondary energy refusing cap100, then
becoming affordable only when both maximum and balance are 200.

Quiet/area are unavailable and are not advertised as usable. Their adapter-specific costs
and any new secondary/material requirements require phase2/stock qualification. This is
an energy-envelope matrix, not proof that all 82 effects, targets, permissions and installed
content are ready.

## Verification boundary

The controlled native fixture verifies raw/effective attribute changes, immediate
down-clamping, no refill, invalid-cap refusal before payment/mastery, focused-instance
canonical ownership, owner-body fixture replacement, route disable/detach/reattach,
repeated enrolment and separate-process reconstruction. Its live-service effective reload
restores raw10 plus saved bonuses3+7 and preserves balance150/max200 until complete;
removing the bonuses then clamps to balance100/max100.

The fixture exercises the production restoration scope, real Character/Body/Attribute
objects, builders, command adapter, MySQL persistence and independent readers. Full
Character login, actual form/backup commands, installed-world Telnet, stock mapping,
all gathering-method restrictions and per-effect stock qualification are **not run**.
N08 therefore remains partial. Actual receipts/fingerprints and earlier failures are
retained in `Armageddon_Stage1C_Verification.json` and the ignored local test artifacts.
