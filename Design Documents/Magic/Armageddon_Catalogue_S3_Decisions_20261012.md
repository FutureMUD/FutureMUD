Preserve exact infection-instance attribution, bounded duration, ordinary treatment and the no-spontaneous-spread rule.
- The ten tradition foci and two charged-device blank prototypes, including their twelve recipes, move to S4 alongside their real spell dependencies. S3 does not create fabricated whitelist IDs, disabled placeholder spells or incomplete devices. The component blank, focus blank and binding seal remain S3 supplies.
- Food translates the approved source calories into native satiation. At the explicit 2,400-calorie/day reference, each approved 500-calorie meal supplies five satiation hours, five bites and 0.1 litre of water. The calorie number remains source provenance only; no runtime calorie field, persistence model or accounting is added or restored.

The 210 approved blueprint identities, progression values and other stock values remain unchanged. The S3 exit gate covers the catalogue families whose native dependencies are available before S4; the twelve deferred focus/device blueprints and recipes are explicit S4 obligations. Spell hydration, per-instance grade/lifetime bindings and carrier calibration retain their existing S4/S5 gates. Release activation remains disabled.
