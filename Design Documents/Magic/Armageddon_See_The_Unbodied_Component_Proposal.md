## Selection and consumption contract

Source configuration must require a dedicated quantity-one, directly carried Divination consume action with `GradeRank offset=-3` and five explicit hierarchical rank tags0-4. Grades1/2/3 require rank0; grades4/5/6/7 require ranks1/2/3/4. Higher descendants qualify; unrelated goods do not. Low grades never waive the outside-Shadow component. Native consumption of one quantity unit, rather than extraction of an entire historical stack object, is the declared engine adaptation. Other authored payment actions must be preserved; the adapter may omit only the precisely validated dedicated component action.

The existing capability prerequisite builder can express `casting prerequisite add <See spell> <Pierce spell> 1 80`: grade1 is its ordinary acquisition baseline and raw80 belongs to Pierce. Source-shaped efficiency with minimum7 and scale1 charges grade1 **7** and grade7 **50** when the acquired controlled grade is7; the printed minimum is not a flat cost at every grade. No new energy curve is proposed.

Silt refusal precedes the Shadow exemption. Silt and Shadow must bind explicit native terrain definitions, with their source-sector meaning documented; no guessed names, historical integers or implicit plane topology. Use raw cell/overlay/terrain state at commitment, not a callback that can change policy during debit. The exact mapping surface must be checked against native terrain APIs before implementation.

Only an invocation copy receives the selected plan. Preserve the original authored plan XML for configuration equivalence. Deep-clone the selected plan and bind its consumed actions to the already selected grade, since replacing the copy's plan after `CastingCopy` otherwise loses its runtime grade binding. Outside Shadow, retain the component action and compose its primary selector with pure checks of the frozen environment. In Shadow, omit just that component action. Do not mutate the builder's persistent spell or shared plan in either branch.

An immutable prepared token freezes the environment, branch, source scope/configuration, authored plan and grade. `TryReusePreparedSelection` and `TryConfirmPreparedSelection` must reject drift instead of choosing a new branch or substituting inputs. Existing service admission then revalidates the actual selected component identity, custody, quantity and rank before payment; existing native consumption performs the mutation once. No component extraction belongs in eligibility or the effect callback.

## Concrete existing hooks and commitment order

The proposed new effect partial adds `IMagicSpellEffectPreparedSelection` to `DetectEtherealEffect`, with private immutable `SourceEtherealScope` and `SourceEtherealSelection` records. Absent source scope returns a null selection and preserves the verified generic adapter. The owned lifetime partial calls source-scope XML/builder helpers; the owned operation partial calls a pure frozen-environment confirmation before child creation/attachment. No shared factory change is needed for the already registered `detectethereal` effect. The new stock class prepares its source-scoped definition; the optional owned `MagicSpell` builder partial supplies factory construction helpers. Public stock-command/installer registry wiring is returned as an integration note rather than edited here.

1. `MagicCastingService.Prepare` creates a detached `CastingCopy`, binds its source grade, then invokes `CapturePreparedSelection` **before** creating the payment inventory plan. The source partial checks self, explicit Silt/Shadow mappings and the precisely identified dedicated material action. Silt refuses first. The action must have a unique persisted `OriginalReference`, quantity1, carried-only selection and the validated offset/rank hierarchy; ambiguous or duplicate references fail closed.
2. Outside Shadow, a grade-bound deep copy of the authored plan uses existing native `CreatePlan`, `PlanIsFeasible` and **non-executing** `PeekPlanResults` to resolve that dedicated action's `PrimaryTarget` by its `OriginalReference`. Freeze that exact item reference/ID, prototype and relevant raw rank/tag/custody/quantity state in the token. Pin the invocation-local consume selector to that item plus pure frozen-environment/mapping checks. Do not use a parallel manual inventory search. In Shadow the token has no selected Divination item and only that dedicated action is omitted; other authored actions remain. Rebind consumed actions after replacing `InventoryPlanTemplate`. Neither branch executes a plan during capture.
3. `ReusePreparedSelections` invokes `TryReusePreparedSelection` on each fresh invocation copy during live preparation. Reconstruct the same selected plan from the frozen authored configuration, branch and pinned item; never scout a replacement or switch exemptions. Existing plan equivalence also compares the selected input IDs and costs. The original persisted spell/plan remains unchanged.
4. The service re-prepares before incantation and again after its `BeforePayment` callback. `AdmitSelectionPayment` finishes capacity callbacks, calls `TryConfirmPreparedSelection` on the original frozen selection and checks captured raw actor/input/configuration state. These are the last ordinary policy evaluations before commitment. Reject environment, definition, component, rank, quantity or custody drift here before any debit or operation creation.
5. The existing service writes the durable **`Paying`** receipt and opportunity locks, invokes its `Paying` checkpoint and opens exact native debit admission. `MagicSpell.Pay` confirms lifetime membership, debits resources, invokes **`PaymentMutated`**, then executes/finalises its already scouted native plan. `InventoryPlanActionConsume.RevalidateConsumption` runs immediately before native consumption and invokes the pinned pure selector; it confirms identity/environment plus the existing carried/rank/quantity rules. Shadow executes no dedicated Divination consumption. The adapter never debits resources or consumes/refunds an item itself.
6. The existing route flushes and invokes **`Committed`**, then runs the casting check and effect operation. The source operation confirms the frozen environment/configuration immediately before granting perception. This post-consumption confirmation must not require the paid component to remain present or have its old quantity. A Shadow-to-outside move before this operation throws instead of granting an outside cast without its component. Existing typed lifetime finalisation and truthful mutation reports remain authoritative.
7. Failures before durable `Paying` are Refused with no payment operation. Failures from `Paying` onward follow the existing `NeedsReview` journal/quarantine path, even when the exact amount of mutation is uncertain; they do not imply that every debit already happened. Once paid, never substitute components, refund, replay, reroll or grant mastery after uncertainty. Existing retained partial-child bounding/old-cohort preservation applies rather than invented rollback. Completed-origin and uncertain-origin retries are already refused by the service.

This is a design for allocation, not implemented or accepted conditional payment. The non-executing native scouting and frozen selector must be qualified by contract tests, including duplicate action references, component substitution, mapping/tag changes, both environment directions, low grades, payment checkpoints, actual quantity conservation and fresh-process receipts. Ordinary configured casting is the proposed lane; carrier routes remain outside this allocation.

After debit, the effect operation reconfirms the frozen environment before granting perception. It must not require the consumed item to remain present. Outside Shadow, the composed pure selector also checks environment during native consumption revalidation. A post-debit environment or component change follows the existing `NeedsReview` quarantine path: no guessed replacement, free outside-Shadow grant, duplicate consumption, automatic replay or refund. An empty Shadow plan cannot acquire an outside-Shadow effect after a post-debit move.

## Required qualification before stock acceptance

Tests must cover normal builder construction/edit/clone/reload; Pierce raw79 refusal/raw80 acquisition at opening30; all seven consumed rank thresholds; Shadow no-consumption; Silt/self/absent-component refusal before debit; native quantity conservation; custody/environment/configuration drift before payment and after debit; actual cost/application receipts; old-cohort retention on paid uncertainty; no duplicate/refund/replay after restart; and cumulative ethereal expiry/perception with concrete parent/child reload. A separately allocated implementation must demonstrate these against fingerprinted final source and assemblies in lane-unique disposable native fixtures. This proposal itself claims no conditional-payment acceptance.
