# Dispel expiry reporting correction

Independent review of reporting checkpoint `48132987` identified a false positive: a 100-second native schedule with 50 seconds left can be rescheduled with zero shortening and acquire a 50-second original duration. Comparing original duration consequently did not prove an earlier expiry. The prior receipt remains historical test evidence, not approval of that comparison.

Configured Dispel now returns `NoChange` before any mutation when the loaded shortening rounds to zero ticks or is nonpositive. A read-only optional `IEffectExpiryObserver` exposes the existing native schedule's absolute `TriggerETA`; it introduces no clock or parallel timing implementation. The native scheduler implements it in a separate partial. Shortening compares actual expiry boundaries. Native timed-substance shortening continues to compare its stored remaining dose duration; permanent substances and unscheduled ordinary effects cannot qualify merely through countdown.

For a scheduler without the observer, shortening still calls the existing operation once and reports `Unknown` unless actual parent removal proves application. Any observed removal or shortened boundary wins over another unobservable target. This prevents unknown observations concealing a positively proven operation. The durable `applied` flag means **proven intended application**, not a claim that every effect stayed unchanged: an unknown shortening can mutate a timer, remains paid/completed under the existing report contract, and cannot qualify mastery. No receipt schema, replay, snapshot or device behavior changes.

Regressions use real `EffectHandler`, `EffectScheduler`, `EffectSchedule` and an advanced controlled clock. They demonstrate unchanged expiry and no mastery for zero and sub-tick shortening, a genuinely earlier expiry and one mastery sample for positive shortening, and actual mutation with no mastery when the scheduler intentionally hides its observer. Mocked reporting cases additionally cover unobservable no-ops and actual removals. Native paid stock verification is separate.
