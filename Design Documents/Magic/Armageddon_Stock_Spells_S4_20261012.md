## Content and ownership

`ArmageddonStockSpellContent` supplies a shared, typed native-capability/content contribution. `ArmageddonStockSpellInstaller` commits each contribution independently in a Serializable transaction and reports failed, unknown-commit and confirmed-commit/lost-ack outcomes separately. Earlier committed contributions survive a later failure. Fresh-context reconciliation retains the spell and its duration/cost expression IDs. Whole-field three-way reconciliation preserves builder names, descriptions, definitions and expression edits; missing/retired/competing records, lost stock identity and unowned name/identity collisions hold without adoption or resurrection.

Read Attunement uses the Water school, a character-in-room trigger allowing self, no material plan, a `readattunement` child and `60*grade` seconds. Its source efficiency minimum is 20. **20 is the approved curve floor, not a flat invocation price:** at controlled grade one, a grade-one cast costs 50; a controlled-grade-seven caster pays 20 for grades one through five, 25 for six and 50 for seven. Ordinary route policy maps the canonical source cost unit to its selected reserve. External grades 1–7 remain distinct from native powers 2–8. Quiet/formula casting require separate authored incantation policy; this contribution provides ordinary named Say casting and no quiet/area policy. Scroll inscription is false and the existing compatibility contract refuses unqualified frozen carriers.

Classic uses the canonical owned spell skill; Simple uses the native tradition's main skill as the default. Admission-local overrides retain each other tradition's skill/cap/reserve policy. Compatible Classic skills retain their earlier ownership module after S2 stamps the stock progression version. An installed ID does not qualify a roster: this installer never supplies a partial activation map or changes player state.

The effect reads assigned elemental affinities and exact completed enrolment history separately, as described in the [adapter checkpoint](Armageddon_S4_Checkpoint_20261012.md). Its operation interface now reports `Applied` only when it constructs the actual character child, or `Rejected` for an incompatible target. This fixes a live MajorPass which previously saved the overlay but journalled `applied=false`; normal mastery rules still require a successful, applied, eligible next-grade attempt. It introduces no extra metadata reads, grants, rolls or duplicate attachment.
