Compatible Classic skills retain their earlier ownership module after S2 stamps the stock progression version. An installed ID does not qualify a roster: this installer never supplies a partial activation map or changes player state.

The effect reads assigned elemental affinities and exact completed enrolment history separately, as described in the [adapter checkpoint](Armageddon_S4_Checkpoint_20261012.md).
