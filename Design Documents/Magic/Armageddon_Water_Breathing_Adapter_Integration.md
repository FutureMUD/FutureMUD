# Water Breathing source adapter integration

This checkpoint implements the separately authorised adapter after the historical [source qualification](Armageddon_Water_Breathing_Source_Qualification.md) and [ownership allocation proposal](Armageddon_Water_Breathing_Adapter_Ownership_Proposal.md). It is an adapter checkpoint for parent independent review, separate from stock construction and installer contributions. Preserve the earlier receipts as evidence for their original revisions.

## Construction and editable mappings

Use ordinary spell construction and `trigger new character` or `trigger new self`, then `effect add sourcewaterbreathing`. The default accumulated source policy is group `armageddon.water_breathing`, 600 real seconds per unit, cap36. Builders must explicitly configure at least one existing native water-liquid definition with `effect 1 water add <ID or name>`; `water remove` removes a mapping. `effect 1 lifetime <group> <seconds per unit> <maximum units>` edits the cohort and timing policy. Clone, XML save/reload and malformed-policy/scope preservation use the normal template paths.

The spell requires exactly one exclusive target effect, no caster effects, no opposed save or area policy, an ordinary character/self trigger, a seven-grade casting profile and a duration expression whose formula is `0`. That expression is a placeholder for the existing caller. The source duration comes from the prepared selection. Effect configuration and native water IDs remain builder-editable; there is no inferred world liquid, racial identity or stock installation.

`sourcewaterbreathing` constructs `SpellScopedWaterBreathingEffect`, a subtype of the existing `SpellWaterBreathingEffect`. Legacy `waterbreathing` XML retains its historical native broad grant. The subtype participates in the existing `removewaterbreathing` adapter. The new template and child register through existing reflection-based factory discovery; no central registry or shared harness dispatch change is required. The new dedicated harness project supplies its own entrypoint and runner.

## Source selection, accumulation and application

The recovered duration is `max(1, number(floor(grade/2), 3*grade))` source hours with an inclusive sampler. Grade1 really draws0..3; raw0 and raw1 both yield one unit. A private invocation token seals this one draw, source grade, native power, spell/profile version, effect XML, native fluid identities, recipient body/location/layer and both original and potentially reflected caster cohorts. Existing preparation, casting-copy reuse, final payment confirmation and lifetime APIs carry it without redrawing. Practice retains its existing effect-free path.

The allocated typed lifetime seam validates the expected scoped child and exact mappings, native parent identity, grade/power, ownership, policy and observed schedule. Unreadable/mixed/permanent/conflicting cohorts refuse before payment. A scoped child with foreign ownership or an unexpected applicability prog is outside this adapter's admitted metadata. Detection keeps its original expression/child path. Water supplies the selected whole-unit increment to the existing resolver, which sums ceiling-normalised remaining native source units, caps36 and retains the strongest **source grade** with its native power. Grouped source cohorts can span spell/caster IDs; ordinary legacy and differently grouped effects remain independent.

The source operation owns provisional child attachment. Existing parent finalisation verifies attachment, native deadlines and old-cohort cleanup, then reports actual deadline/retained-grade/power change. Replacing references at cap with unchanged strength/deadline is NoChange and does not sample mastery, even when paid. Genuine lifetime extension or grade strengthening can apply to naturally water-breathing recipients. Before-payment drift refuses without an operation or debit; paid callback drift preserves admitted state and existing quarantine rather than recapturing, refunding or replaying.

Exact native expiry and saved remaining-time restoration are explicit engine adaptations of the historical pulse/inclusive-endpoint system. Children have no independent timer or grade authority. The existing native parent persists the lifetime policy/grade, its scoped child saves native liquid IDs, and existing removal/expiry cleans the parent, child and scheduler.

## Breathing and mixture boundary

The allocated compatibility helper preserves normal racial breathing first and admits applicable scoped water grants to lung, gill, blowhole and partless strategies. Only the lung caller opts into the pre-existing broad additional-fluid grants; those grants are not expanded to the other strategies. NonBreather and native respiratory requirement decisions remain unchanged. Existing organs, working parts, airway bleeding, anaesthesia, stop-breathing behavior, actual supply withdrawal, exposure and held-breath updates stay in their existing consumers.

Mappings match exact native ILiquid references and persisted IDs. Gases, null, unmapped liquids and unselected CountsAs aliases receive no additional compatibility. Builders must map distinct fresh/salt-water definitions explicitly. `LiquidMixture` is not an IFluid in this engine: a container mixture does not become the terrain breathing fluid, confer a grant to its constituents or bypass scope. Terrain water is one authored native liquid definition; an explicitly mapped mixture-like liquid definition is admitted by its own identity. Oxygen compatibility is not toxin immunity, organ repair, gas conservation bypass or a survival guarantee in hazardous water/air.
