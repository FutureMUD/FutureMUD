## Strategy and fluid semantics

Lung, blowhole, gill and partless strategies all consume a current fluid and call the shared compatibility helper. All four should honor the explicit source liquid grant: the source character effect has no species/anatomy exclusion. Keep each strategy's existing physiological gates and its native choice of underwater terrain fluid versus atmosphere/supply. Normal racial air/fluid compatibility remains independent and unchanged. NonBreather needs no grant and stays untouched. The bounded helper edit does not give gills a new ability to breathe air or repair a pulmonary airway; it only resolves an authored water compatibility case before existing gates.

Source configuration requires explicitly authored native water-liquid IDs. Initially match only those catalogue references/IDs; do not implicitly grant all liquids, gases, null, or an unselected hazardous fluid because it happens to claim `CountsAs(water)`. Builders can list their intended fresh/salt-water definitions explicitly. No water ID/name is guessed. Toxicity/exposure and organ function remain their existing systems; oxygen compatibility is not toxin immunity. Legacy unconfigured `waterbreathing` definitions retain their old meaning and are not represented as source-qualified content. An automatic legacy migration would be a separate explicit decision, not part of this adapter repair.

No new source gameplay default needs Luke at this point: grade1 draws0..3 then clamps, source units600/cap36 and strongest-grade recast are recovered rules. Actual world fluid identities are required builder bindings. If broader liquid alias rules or an automatic legacy migration are requested, identify that choice rather than assigning it silently. Emotional endurance/attack-break decisions remain unrelated and pending.

## Reuse of cleared preparation/lifetime mechanisms

Current preparation invokes `ValidateLifetimeInvocation` before `CapturePreparedSelection`. The Water increment getter must lazily create its immutable selection token on that first validation; later capture/reuse/confirmation must return or validate the same choice without another draw. The token includes original and potential reflected-recipient cohorts, fluid catalogue references/IDs and policy/grade/power/configuration bindings. Fresh copies receive it through existing `ReusePreparedSelections`; final payment uses existing selection confirmation and callback-free structural fence. After-payment callbacks validate the admitted cohort again before mutation. A changing scope, body, expiry, group, profile, parent/child or catalogue object refuses/quarantines rather than recapturing or refunding.

The lifetime family extension must explicitly match expected kind and scope, not infer kind from a mutable group string. Reject mixed/unknown children or mismatched scoped policy before payment. Detection keeps its exact old expression and child-validation path. Water supplies the already-selected positive whole-unit increment; the same existing resolver sums normalized native remaining units, caps36, retains strongest source grade/native power and proves parent/child/schedule cleanup. Parent lifetime state persists grade; do not add an independent expiry or duplicate grade authority. A SourceWater token does not enable carriers/areas by itself.

The operation may prove a provisional scoped child was retained, but final configured application/mastery must use the existing final observed deadline/retained-grade result. At a full cap with unchanged grade/power/scope, replacing references alone must remain NoChange. Real lifetime extension or grade strengthening can be Applied even if a naturally water-breathing target's current respiration is already valid. Paid uncertainty remains existing quarantine; no newly allocated child or sampled duration is an acceptance receipt.

## Qualification boundary

The independent values verify source bounds, grade1's real draw, native-power separation, binding/immutable reads and explicit fluid reference scope. They do not prove repeated service preparation, paid operations, native strategy consumption or persistence. After allocation, dedicated adapter tests must cover sealed repetition/reflection/callback drift, strong/weak recasts and cap/no-change, paid application reports, all four strategies with physiology failures and ordinary air retained, toxic gas/unselected liquid/null rejection, generic removal, parent expiry/dispel and independent-process reload. Use a dedicated adapter native entrypoint with its own database/process/evidence; qualify it before authoring the separate stock definition. Preserve every prior receipt and regression.
