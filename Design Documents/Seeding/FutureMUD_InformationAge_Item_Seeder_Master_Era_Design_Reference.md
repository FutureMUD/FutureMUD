# FutureMUD Information Age ItemSeeder Master Era Design Reference

## Contract

`information` is the canonical ItemSeeder key. `computer` remains a compatibility alias and the internal VehicleSeeder token. The era tag is `Era / Information Age Era`, the manifest module is `information`, and era-specific stable references begin `information_`.

The era remains non-selectable until real stock, profile mappings and tests exist.

## Scope

The Information delta target is 850 ordinary prototypes. It represents personal and portable computing, data networks, digital media, contemporary retail and logistics, modern access control, connected offices and institutions, and late-model electrical/vehicle support.

Priority domains are:

- desktop, portable and embedded computers with peripherals and removable media;
- network infrastructure, cabling, telephony, storage, displays, cameras and audio/video systems;
- keycards, biometric access, alarms, scanners and contemporary security hardware;
- digital office, point-of-sale, banking/payment, warehouse and delivery equipment;
- contemporary medical, laboratory, emergency and accessibility equipment;
- chargers, batteries, adapters, power distribution and vehicle-service connectors;
- domestic electronics, appliances, recreation and personal carry goods.

## Meaningful interaction rule

Information-era goods create the greatest risk of decorative overclaim. Use existing computer, network, media, sensor, camera, grid and telephone components where they model the interaction. Add reusable missing prototypes through UsefulSeeder. A generic powered prop is acceptable for switch/state presentation, not as a substitute for promised networking, scanning, authentication, payment or data behaviour.

## Boundaries

Do not use real companies or product lines. Do not seed cloud services, websites or software as physical items. Laser weapons, neural interfaces and implant families are future-specialist content unless a concrete runtime-backed domain is separately approved. Aircraft, submarines and free-coordinate vehicles remain outside the standard vehicle catalogue.

## Activation gate

Activation requires exact technology-profile mappings, component behaviour parity with prose, populated domain and executable manifests, dependency and repeatability evidence, full DatabaseSeeder tests, fresh and populated database replay, generated export parity and representative live-MUD inspection. Actual counts and deferred behaviours must be published in the activation review.
