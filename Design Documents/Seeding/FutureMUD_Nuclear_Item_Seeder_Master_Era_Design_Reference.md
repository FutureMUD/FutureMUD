# FutureMUD Nuclear ItemSeeder Master Era Design Reference

## Contract

`nuclear` is the canonical ItemSeeder key. `atomic` remains a compatibility alias and the internal VehicleSeeder token. The era tag is `Era / Nuclear Era`, the manifest module is `nuclear`, and era-specific stable references begin `nuclear_`.

The name denotes the post-war electronics and advanced industrial period; it is not a mandate to seed speculative nuclear machinery. The era remains non-selectable until populated and validated.

## Scope

The Nuclear delta target is 800 ordinary prototypes. It covers transistorised and early integrated electronics, mature aviation-adjacent ground support, institutional computing, advanced laboratories and hospitals, post-war consumer goods, modernised civil defence and high-throughput industry.

Priority domains are:

- transistor radio, television, audio recording, cameras and mature media distribution;
- mainframe/minicomputer rooms, terminals, data media and office-machine support;
- advanced diagnostic, laboratory, clean-room and hospital equipment;
- industrial controls, sensors, test instruments and improved protective equipment;
- post-war domestic, leisure, travel, education and institutional stock;
- controlled substances, signalling and explosive-support goods with appropriate access metadata;
- vehicle electronics and service tools supported by existing runtime behaviour.

## Boundaries

Ordinary radioactivity props may be seeded only with accurate material and hazard mechanics. Reactors, radiation systems, guided weapons and aircraft are excluded until their runtime designs exist. Personal networked computing belongs to Information. Cybernetic implants remain deferred unless a concrete supported domain justifies them.

## Activation gate

Activation requires all delta dependencies and hazards to be source-backed, technology-profile compatibility to be explicit, no inert item to promise unsupported sensing/control/radiation behaviour, and the same manifest, repeatability, full-test, database-replay, export and live-inspection evidence required by the programme.
