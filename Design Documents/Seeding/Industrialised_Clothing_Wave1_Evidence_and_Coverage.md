### Approved scope inclusions and deferrals

- Include ordinary clothes across all four bands in the design inventory; implement exact shared/Industrial admissions first. Subsequent era activations remain separate milestones.
- Include common infant, school, maternity and assisted-dressing constructions, without multiplying all adult bases into nominal age/size versions.
- Include named regional and religious forms when the garment name is normal English usage; avoid replacing their actual construction with culturally generic skins.
- Include camouflage patterns as presentation; grant no concealment effect without an actual supported mechanism. Include work/medical clothing without claiming chemical, sterile, ballistic or fire protection from prose.
- Reference separately owned PPE/armour, jewellery, ritual implements, carried equipment and weapons. This package does not duplicate those item systems or present bare clothing as a complete safety loadout.
- Defer aircraft/space/diving pressure suits, powered exosuits, active camouflage, smart garments requiring electronics, true automated factories and new tailoring/body-shaping subsystems. Ordinary garment appearances cannot stand in for those functions.
- Defer functional child-carrying garments such as an amauti until carrying and wear relationships are proved; an ordinary parka skin must not erase or falsely promise that function.
- Defer exhaustive rank-by-country, brand, model-year, nominal size and purely colour-generated matrices. This is not a deferral of ordinary uniform functions or representative regional garments.
- Exclude forced-deformation footwear and speculative universal ethnic/religious uniforms from the ordinary stock goal; historically important examples may be reconsidered only with explicit scope and mechanics review.
