# Industrialised clothing — Wave 1 garment inventory

## Contract and reading key

This is the approved scope inventory under the [clothing specification](./FutureMUD_Industrialised_Clothing_Footwear_Uniforms_Design_Reference.md), not executable item source or finished descriptions. The user approved its counts and overall scope on 2026-09-03, with conventional colours assigned to outfit defaults; see the evidence/coverage approval record. Infrastructure and dependency Gate 2 passed on 2026-09-04; content and production Gates 3–7 remain open. Do not generate prose from the briefs below.

Each table row represents one physical/economic base design, not a colour, size, profession or country variant. `Key` is its unique planning reference. For `new` rows, reserve `<prefix>clothing_<Key>`: `industrialised_` for two or more admitted eras, otherwise the sole canonical era prefix. For reuse rows, `Source` is the exact existing identity; never rename it to the planning key.

Era letters expand to exact admissions in chronological order: `I=industrial`, `M=modern`, `N=nuclear`, `F=information`. They propose availability in those technology bands, not a universal calendar or an assertion that all cultures wore the garment. Narrow historical cuts are deliberately not made universal. Object-level date/material verification remains Gate 4 evidence work; a correction must update affected outfits and counts, not silently widen admissions.

Routes: `H=hand construction`, `S=machine-assisted individual construction`, `B=standardised batch manufacture`. Each base/default and its listed skins use that route unless a later explicitly approved exception says otherwise. A batch craft is an attended, supported recipe, not factory automation. `Skin briefs` are individually chosen presentation designs separated by semicolons; `-` means no additional planned skin. Briefs never authorise material, cut, quality, pocket or price changes. All support variable colour; conventional colours are overridable outfit defaults. No fixed-colour exception is approved in this inventory.

Every new base and every additional skin is planned to receive one normal production recipe. These are recipe commitments, not completed crafts; reuse defaults retain existing production if valid, and new skin-specific products require a new recipe. The checker reports new-base recipes, skin-product recipes and reused-default obligations separately so nothing is counted as implemented or twice. Later waves may consolidate actual recipe selectors only with an explicit count reconciliation. A plain default is not counted again as a plain skin; additional briefs must introduce an actual compatible presentation distinction.

Explicit user requirement: the plain/default unskinned version is itself a finished, viable garment for stocking, crafting, wearing and outfit use, not merely a template awaiting a skin. Every row therefore includes one complete unskinned presentation in its base count. It needs its own finished prose, valid colour configuration, normal production route, physical behaviour and price/quality. A presentation previously planned as a plain skin may instead supply that base presentation and is removed from the additional-skin count. An empty skin list is entirely valid for a standalone garment; variable colour applies to every base in this approved scope.

Evidence codes identify research workstreams in the evidence register, not extracted prices or final date proof. Physical distinction briefs are design decisions for review, not copied museum descriptions. Existing identities are checked against both maintained manifest entries and live source definitions; that verifies existence, not later-era readiness, price, colour or prose acceptance.
