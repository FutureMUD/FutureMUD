### Pith material prerequisite

CoreDataSeeder now supplies `pith` for `industrialised_clothing_pith_helmet`, with the aliases `shola pith`, `sola pith` and `sholapith`. The existing exact tag paths are `Materials / Natural Materials / Wood` and `Functions / Material Functions / Primary Production / Primary Production Commodity`; no taxonomy branch or finished helmet is introduced.

The material identity and identified consumer are supported by Bortolaso's discussion of sholapith (*Aeschynomene aspera*) in [BfN-Skripten 228, page 32, section 3.2](https://www.bfn.de/sites/default/files/BfN/service/Dokumente/skripten/skript228.pdf#page=33), which describes the soft stem material and its historical use for hats. [Mandal, Bar and Chattopadhyay, Indian Journal of Traditional Knowledge 13(1), 2014, pages 103–110](https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/handle/123456789/26032?mode=full) additionally describes its soft, spongy working properties. These establish a lightweight plant material, not impact protection or a claim that every historical sun helmet used this core.

The seeded density is 40 kg/m³, using the *Aeschynomene* specific-gravity value 0.04 in [IGNOU Economic Botany, Unit 20, density discussion](https://www.egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/16758/1/Unit-20.pdf) as an explicitly **genus-level analogue**, not a measured value for *A. aspera* helmet stock. The remaining seed arguments—shear/impact yield 1,000, absorbency 1.0, thermal conductivity 0.06, electrical conductivity 0.0001 and specific heat 1,300—are **gameplay calibration estimates**, not measurements from those sources. They represent fragile, absorbent, low-conductivity stock through existing material behaviour. Garment protection, weather handling, final weight and pricing still need their own component/evidence review; this material does not close those obligations.

| `craft-tools.tsv` | Ordered exact tag, held/wielded/in-room state, quality weight and tool-duration policy |
| `craft-products.tsv` | Ordered success/failure item, commodity or unused-input outputs; exact item/skin and optional material-defining input |
| `craft-colours.tsv` | Every item-product channel selects either an exact value or a one-based source input, never an implicit random result |

Quantities are counts for items/tagged items, grams for solid commodities, millilitres for liquid inputs, and a fraction in `(0, 1]` for unused-input recovery. The persistence adapter converts through established unit helpers, not assumed engine base units. Recovery percentages scale the authored decimal fraction without binary rounding drift. Item output counts must be integers. `UnusedInput` currently reconstructs item counts, not commodity/liquid mass; normalized recipes reject such mass recovery and heterogeneous multi-item tagged recovery. Declare supported, truthful outputs instead. This restriction does not resolve all required waste/recovery designs. Craft input colour transmission and mixed fixed/inherited channels require runtime-backed proof, not just a parsed input index.

`Hand`, `MachineAssisted` and `Batch` are normal production routes; batch means an attended supported craft, not factory automation. `Draft` and `Reviewed` are editorial states, not milestone completion. No stock skin quality override is supplied by default. Skin colour rows may narrow an inherited palette but may not invent characteristic definitions or profiles on the base.
