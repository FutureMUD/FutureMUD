FutureMUD now has a searchable section for the guides you use while building in game. You can search by command or topic, or choose a category to see what's available. The first 16 guides cover room and area building, character creation, item authoring, crafting and projects, agriculture, health and exposure, magic, and staged performances. [Browse the builder guides](/docs/builder-guides)

If you're starting an area from scratch, the room-building guide takes you through your first overlay package, exits, and room descriptions. For a larger area, the autobuilder guide shows how to choose templates and bring in a map from Terrain Planner. [Room building](/docs/builder-guides/rooms) · [Autobuilder](/docs/builder-guides/autobuilder)

The guides explain how to put features together in a running game. The command, FutureProg, and item component references are still available when you need an exact command, function signature, or component setting. [Commands](/docs/commands) · [FutureProg functions](/docs/futureprog/functions) · [Item components](/docs/items/components)

Future additions will cover more of the larger systems, including NPCs and AI, combat, medicine, and the economy. We'll also add more complete examples to existing guides so builders can set something up, test it, and see what to check when it behaves differently than expected.
