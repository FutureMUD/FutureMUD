title: FutureMUD Discord Bot 1.8.0
summary: Browse game landmarks and follow court, NPC, storyteller and economy activity from Discord.
date: 2026-11-25
tags: discord-bot, release, staff
---

- **Landmarks.** Use `/game landmarks` to browse and `/game landmark` to inspect a room’s description, terrain and meeting-place or safe-quit status. Public entries are available without linking an account. Verified JuniorAdmins and above can inspect all landmarks and their extra text.
- **Justice.** Linked JuniorAdmins and above can use `/justice status` to see waiting defendants, available judges and active patrols. Each jurisdiction’s configured channel receives trial requests, hearing starts and execution patrol launches. New account announcements go to the bot’s admin channel.
- **NPCs and storytellers.** SeniorAdmins and above can inspect AI assignments with `/npc overview` and group rosters with `/npc group`. Use `/storyteller overview` or `/storyteller status` to inspect queues, usage and recent failures. Pausing and resuming require confirmation. Pausing cancels the active request and discards queued work.
- **Economy alerts.** Linked Admins and above can opt in with `/economy digest enabled:true` to receive DMs when reserves fall short or a scheduled snapshot is more than ten minutes late. A continuing incident is reported once. Run `/economy digest` to check your subscription or use `enabled:false` to turn it off.

Install Bot 1.8.0 before Engine 2.19.0. The new commands use the upgraded Engine and the linked account’s in-game permissions. Give the bot permission to send messages in the admin and jurisdiction channels that should receive announcements.
