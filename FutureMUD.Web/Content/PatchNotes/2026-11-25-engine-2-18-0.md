---
title: FutureMUD Engine 2.18.0
summary: Adds lending, clan governance and staged performances, and improves account recovery, item custody and large-world startup.
date: 2026-11-25
tags: engine, release, gameplay, upgrade
---

**Before upgrading**, install .NET 11, stop the old Engine and back up your MySQL 8.0 database and configuration together. This release uses EF Core 11 with the Microting MySQL provider and adds 11 migrations covering clans, Discord operations, lending, performances, credentials, employment approvals, item deletion and sentencing. Rehearse the upgrade on a copy of your world. If your game uses Discord, update to Bot 1.7.0 first. The old bot cannot read the new Engine’s messages.

- **Lending and employment.** Banks and shops can offer loans with configurable terms, collateral and automatic payments. Pawn appraisals, merchandise prices and stock calculations retain their values across the runtime upgrade. NPCs can seek jobs and take permanent assignments. Employment approvals survive restarts. Staff can inspect and reconcile an interrupted payment before authorising a retry.
- **Clans and elections.** Builders can configure how members and officeholders are addressed, confirm or recall elected officers, accept resignations and use indefinite tenure. Template clans keep their elections dormant while a builder stages a copy for activation.
- **Performances and rites.** Players and staff can author a work, cast players and NPCs, book a venue and run a service, play or concert. Stage links carry the parts a spectator can see or hear into audience rooms. An organiser can inspect a missed cue or replace an absent performer.
- **Items and magic.** Builders have new policies for curses, ethereal items and glyphs. Items that morph or are destroyed place their replacements more reliably. Items held in auction escrow now suspend and resume their effects. Deletion records its progress across restarts and flags uncertain callback outcomes for reconciliation.
- **Accounts and service reliability.** Existing passwords continue to work and upgrade to Argon2id on a successful password login. New passwords accept long passphrases of 15–128 characters. Recovery codes expire and are attempt-limited. Request a fresh code if one was issued before the upgrade, and update any customised eight-character password prompt. Password-change emails no longer include the password. OpenAI request failures now end the affected request without blocking the game loop.
- **Building and running a world.** Fixed grazing on inherited terrain, field project starts, stockroom restocking trips, prospecting samples, successful craft repairs and offsite project shifts. Personal project focus can unlock and preserve earned skill mastery. Item description markup, market elasticity editing, lawyer selection and manual sentencing also received fixes. Builders can look up worked examples for these systems in the searchable [builder guides](https://futuremud.com/docs/builder-guides).
- **Character merits.** Edits to chargen resource costs, spent or required status, and combo-merit parents now survive a save and reload.
- **Large-world startup.** On one large Windows test world, time from launching the process to a responsive menu fell from 196 to 115 seconds after reducing database reads. World size and host hardware affect the result. [Startup measurements](https://github.com/FutureMUD/FutureMUD/blob/12729b8f1/Design%20Documents/Core/Startup_Performance_Investigation_20261113.md)
